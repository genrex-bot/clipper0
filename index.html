FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — entirely. There's no paid tier. The video engine runs in your browser, so there are no server costs to pass on.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Processing happens locally with WebAssembly. Your file never leaves your device, which also means it works offline.

Can I paste a YouTube link instead?

Not yet. Downloading from YouTube requires a server, and this site is hosted for free as static files. Clip downloaded videos instead — tools like yt-dlp can save them locally first.

What formats are supported?

Anything your browser can read and ffmpeg.wasm can decode — MP4, WebM, MOV and most others. Exports are MP4 (H.264/AAC).

Why are cut points not frame-exact?

For speed, v1 cuts at keyframes (typically within a second). Frame-accurate re-encoding is on the roadmap.
